# Cell biology notes (week 3)

## Mitochondria

Mitochondria are the cell's ATP factories. They run cellular respiration: glucose and oxygen go in, and ATP, carbon
dioxide and water come out. Our teacher calls the inner membrane folds "cristae" and says more cristae means more
surface for making ATP. Muscle cells have many mitochondria because they need a lot of energy.

## Chloroplasts

Chloroplasts are only in plant cells and algae. They do photosynthesis: light reactions in the thylakoids, then the
Calvin cycle in the stroma, which turns carbon dioxide into sugar.

## The cell membrane

The membrane is a phospholipid bilayer. Small non-polar molecules cross it by simple diffusion; ions need channel
proteins. Exam tip from class: active transport uses ATP to move things against their concentration gradient.
